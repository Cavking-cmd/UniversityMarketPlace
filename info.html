Terms of Service

last updated: september 2026

UniManitoba.store is a listings board that connects students who want to buy, sell, or swap goods and services. We don't handle payments, shipping, or hold onto anything you list — every trade happens directly between the two of you.

  • You're responsible for the accuracy of your own listings and for showing up to meetups you agree to.
  • No stolen goods, counterfeit items, prohibited substances, or anything you wouldn't be comfortable handing over in a public campus space.
  • We can remove a listing or account that breaks these terms or the community guidelines below.
  • This service is provided as-is, with no guarantee that any listing, buyer, or seller is exactly as described — use your judgment.

Privacy Policy

last updated: september 2026

We keep the data we collect to the minimum needed to run the board:

  • Your U of M email, so buyers and sellers can reach each other directly.
  • What you post in a listing — title, price, description, category, and pickup spot.
  • Basic usage data (like which pages get visited) to keep the board working and spot abuse.

We don't sell your information to advertisers or third parties. You can ask to have your listings or account data removed at any time.

Community guidelines

last updated: september 2026

  • Be straightforward — accurate photos, honest condition, real prices.
  • Meet in busy, public, well-lit spots on campus (see the meetup spots on the homepage).
  • No harassment, discrimination, or pressuring anyone to meet somewhere private.
  • Report a listing or user that feels off — we'd rather look into a false alarm than miss a real one.
