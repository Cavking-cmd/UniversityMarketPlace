How it works

Three steps, no middleman, no fees.

1

Post what you've got

Snap a photo, set a price, pick a category. Takes under two minutes.

2

Chat with a buyer or seller

Messages stay inside the app, tied to your U of M email, so it's students only.

3

Meet somewhere on campus

Trade cash for goods at a public, well-lit spot. Mark the listing sold when you're done.

Good spots to meet up

Stick to busy, public places on campus — here are a few students use most.

University Centre

Central and always busy, good for daytime swaps.

Elizabeth Dafoe Library

Quiet corners near the entrance, open late during exams.

Active Living Centre lobby

Good for bikes and gear — wide doors, easy to carry stuff in.

UMSU Student Bookstore

The obvious pick for textbook handoffs at start of term.
